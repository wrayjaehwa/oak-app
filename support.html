Support

Oak Support

For account assistance, technical issues, safety concerns, or questions about Oak, reach out to our team and we will help direct your request.

Contact Support

For account assistance, technical issues, safety concerns, or questions about Oak, contact: info@oakco.app

We aim to respond to safety and content reports within 24 hours.

What To Include

To help us review your request faster, include the email tied to your account, the device you are using, and a short description of the issue or concern.

Safety and Content Reports

If you are reporting harmful content, impersonation, harassment, or another safety issue, please include links, screenshots, usernames, and any relevant context so our team can investigate.

Technical Issues

If something in Oak is not working as expected, tell us what happened, when it happened, and what steps you took before the issue appeared. This makes it easier for us to reproduce and fix problems.

General Questions

For general support or partnership questions, email info@oakco.app and we will route your message to the right place.
